## Characters (CC0 1.0, public domain)

By [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/): *Ultimate Modular Men* and *Ultimate Modular Women* packs (outfits Beach, Suit,
Worker; Casual, Formal, Suit, Worker). Converted to GLB keeping only the Idle, Idle_Neutral, Walk, Run, Wave and Interact
animations, and merged into one vertex-coloured mesh per character at load time.

## Buildings and street props (CC0 1.0, public domain)

From Quaternius' [Downtown City MegaKit](https://quaternius.itch.io/downtown-city-megakit) (free Standard version):
Building_Small_1, Building_Medium_2_001, Building_Large_2, Prop_Planter_Single, Prop_Bollard, Prop_ManholeCover and
Prop_Drain, with their textures resized to 1024 px JPEG in `assets/kit/`.
